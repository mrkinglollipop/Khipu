### Identity rule

`packages/cli/khipu/routing_identity.py:59` uses a label slug only when it
cannot be the normalized login basename (including discover.default_name's
provider-prefix removal and numbered-name suffix). Correlated labels and
legacy colliding slugs receive provider-scoped opaque allocations,
`<provider>:account-<number>` (`:78`, `:90`). Their number comes from reserved
identity allocations, never a basename or a hash of a login path.
Compatibility forwards accept previously exposed folder-derived stable ids.
The ordinary owner rename path can replace the opaque identity and preserves
its old aliases. This deliberately treats even a manually supplied label
that matches the login basename conservatively.
