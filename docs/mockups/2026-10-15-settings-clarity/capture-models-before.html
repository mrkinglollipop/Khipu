Models

Session summaries follow this card: an API key (Gemini, Claude Haiku, OpenAI, OpenRouter), the Claude or ChatGPT login you already use, or a local OpenAI-compatible endpoint. The monthly topic pass runs its own model and is not switched here. The model that searches by meaning is chosen under Embeddings.

Session summaries

Claude Haiku 5.5: the best memory, first in 10 of 15 judged windows, 3 wrong or invented items, about 11 s a capture.

GPT-6 Luna: accurate where it answers (2 wrong items) but thin on open loops; about 8 s a capture at low effort, 50 s at high.

Gemini 2.5 Flash: the fastest, about 3 s, and last on quality: no first places, about 10 wrong or invented items.

Measured 2026-10-13 on 15 real windows. Gemini stays the embedder for search by meaning whichever summariser you pick.

Accounts readDefault

Every installed CLI is read with its default login. Add an account to read a second login, such as another Claude or Grok sign-in.

The login folder is where that CLI keeps a second sign-in; leave it empty for the default login. With no Name, the account is named after its CLI and login folder.
