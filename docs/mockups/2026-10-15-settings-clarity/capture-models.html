Khipu saves a short summary of each conversation (a capture) and keeps a list of what conversations say is still owed (commitments). Here you choose when it saves, which model writes the summaries, and how commitments close.

When Khipu saves a conversation

After 5 turns or 20 minutes, before a conversation is compacted, and when it ends. The same on every harness.

Keys, tokens and passwords are masked before anything is summarised or stored.

The capture script writes files and Khipu stores each capture too.

From 0 to 1; higher merges only closer matches. A word matching rule can also merge close captures. Default 0.92.

Commitments

A commitment is something a conversation said is still owed: a follow-up, a blocker, a question or a promise. They are listed on Owed.

Names that mean you when Khipu decides who owns a commitment. Separate them with commas. Default: none.

From 0 to 1. A word and completion rule can also close a commitment. Default 0.85.
